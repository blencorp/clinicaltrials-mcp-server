## Tips

- **Pass `fields` on every search.** A full study record is large.
- **Use large pages of few fields.** Every page is one call against the budget of 100 for one run,
  so a page of 200 to 500 studies with three fields beats many small pages.
- **Ask for `countTotal` on the first page only.** It adds `totalCount`.
- **Set a page limit.** A loop that follows `nextPageToken` ends when the token is missing, and a
  limit keeps a broad search from spending the budget.
- **Return less.** A returned value over 256 KiB fails with `ResultTooLarge`.
- **No matches is not an error.** A search with no matches returns a page with no studies.

The [SDK reference](/reference/sdk/) lists every method.
